# Design direction

## World

Executive dossier and operational briefing: restrained editorial typography, dark operational fields, warm signal color, visible evidence, and generous reading space. The page should feel like an advisory document that can be acted on, not a generic SaaS landing page.

## Surface strategy

Three tabs expose the same professional proposition through distinct but compatible evaluation lenses:

1. **Executivo** — advisory positioning and evidence, informed by `design-orchestration`.
2. **Operação** — diagnostic method and role fit, informed by `frontend-design`.
3. **Carreira** — factual career/candidature overview, informed by `mobile-design` and `web-design-guidelines`.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#111b27`
- Paper: `#f5f2eb`
- Signal: `#cf5b3d`
- Operational blue: `#1d5263`
- Display: Playfair Display
- Body: DM Sans
- Metadata: DM Mono

## Constraints

- No invented employers, dates, education, client outcomes, contact details, or testimonials.
- Every tab exposes the assisting skill/agent at upper right.
- Tabs must work by keyboard and on small screens.
