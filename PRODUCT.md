# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The deliverable is a portable, immediately viewable personal site without a build dependency.

## Users

Owners and executives of medium-sized companies, technology leaders, and recruiters evaluating Jeiel Borner for senior IT leadership or advisory work.

## Product Purpose

Present an evidence-led professional narrative for Jeiel Borner: executive IT advisory for critical operations and candidacy for Head of IT / specialist consulting roles.

## Positioning

Technology-critical operations need diagnosis, priority, governance, and safe execution — not disconnected tools or ungoverned AI initiatives.

## Operating Context

The site is read during executive hiring and advisory evaluation. Visitors need a fast explanation of the value proposition, credible operational evidence, a concise career narrative, and relevant role fit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site contains three tabs with the same factual narrative interpreted through different design approaches for evaluation.
- Skill/agent provenance must be visible in the upper-right of every tab.
- Career facts must be grounded in previous conversation evidence; missing dates, education, contact information, and unverified applications remain editable placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

Jeiel Borner Consultoria em TI. Voice: sober, executive, practical, technically credible, without exaggerated claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- National ERP expansion from the Northeast to 29 distribution centers and 435 stores.
- Leadership of teams with 30+ professionals.
- SLA evolution reported as 70%→92% and 80%→96%.
- Experience cited with Walmart, Grupo Big and Carrefour; ERP, SAP, TOTVS, logistics, CRM, EDI, Data Lake, migration, suppliers, security, data, automation and governed AI.
- Applications/candidacy work evidenced for IT Cluster Head Brazil and Gerente de Sistemas at Vision One.

## Product Principles

- Lead with business risk and operational continuity, not tools.
- Distinguish verified evidence from positioning claims.
- Make the next executive conversation easy to start.
- Treat AI as governed operational capability, not fashion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible tabs, visible focus state, semantic headings, responsive layout, and contrast suitable for reading.
